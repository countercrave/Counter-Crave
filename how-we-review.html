How We Review and Recommend Products

Our methodology emphasizes fit, constraints and transparent research instead of invented lab scores.

1. Define the buyer

Every guide starts with a use case: household size, batch size, available space, cleanup tolerance and budget.

2. Build a relevant shortlist

We include products that represent meaningfully different formats or price tiers, not a long list of near-identical models.

3. Verify the current listing

Model names, ASINs and included accessories should be checked before publication. Because listings change, readers are also told to verify details at checkout.

4. Explain buy-if / skip-if

Recommendations are written as decisions. A product can be excellent and still be wrong for a small counter, a large family or a specific workflow.

5. Refresh instead of pretending prices are fixed

We link to Amazon for current price and stock instead of hard-coding a price that may be wrong tomorrow.
