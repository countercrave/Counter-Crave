How to Clean and Maintain an Air Fryer: The Complete Care Guide

A practical after-use routine, deep-cleaning schedule and part-replacement plan that keeps an air fryer performing like new for years.

Updated 2026-08-18 · Research-led editorial guide · As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Quick answer: Wash the parts food touches after every use, keep the powered body dry and wipe-only, and put the deeper jobs on a monthly calendar. Most air fryers "failures" are maintenance debts coming due.

Most air fryer problems people blame on the machine—white smoke, sticky baskets, food that tastes faintly of last week's wings—are really cleaning problems. Grease that drips below the crisper plate carbonizes on the next run and smokes. Coating that gets scraped or sprayed with the wrong product starts shedding. A five-minute routine after cooking keeps the appliance performing like new for years.

The after-every-use routine

1. Empty and cool before touching anything

Unplug the unit and let the basket cool until warm, not hot. Cleaning a screaming-hot nonstick basket with cold water can warp thin metal and stress the coating.

2. Wash the basket and crisper plate after every use

Warm water, a drop of dish soap, and a soft sponge is all it takes when grease has not baked on. Soak stubborn spots for ten minutes instead of scrubbing with anything abrasive.

3. Wipe the cavity and the heating element area

Once fully cool, wipe the inside walls and the area under the coil with a damp cloth. Grease film here is what turns into smoke on your next batch of fries.

4. Dry everything completely

Trapped moisture between the basket and drawer promotes corrosion at the coating's edges. Air-dry upside down or towel-dry before reassembling.

5. Wipe the exterior and control panel

A damp microfiber cloth keeps grease from creeping into buttons and dials, where it eventually makes controls sticky or unresponsive.

Deep-cleaning jobs worth calendaring

Degrease the heating coil monthly

With the unit unplugged and cool, turn it over or open the cavity and go over the coil area with a soft brush or a cloth barely dampened with warm soapy water. Never soak or spray the coil directly.

Reset a sticky basket with a baking-soda soak

For polymerized grease that soap won't lift, cover it with a paste of baking soda and water for 20 to 30 minutes, then wipe gently. Skip steel wool and scouring powder entirely—they end nonstick coatings fast.

Check the drawer channel and vents

Crumbs collect in the rails the drawer slides on and in the rear exhaust vent. A dry brush or a vacuum crevice tool keeps airflow, and therefore crisping performance, at full strength.

What quietly ruins air fryers

Aerosol cooking sprays

Many aerosol sprays contain lecithin and propellants that polymerize into a sticky varnish standard nonstick coatings cannot shed. Use an oil mister or brush oil on the food instead of the basket.

Metal utensils and abrasive pads

One session with steel wool can remove more coating than a year of cooking. Silicone, wood, and nylon only.

Dishwasher-by-default

Even when the basket is labeled dishwasher safe, high heat and harsh detergent age coatings faster. Hand washing meaningfully extends coating life.

Submerging the main unit

The drawer and plate are washable; the body with the motor, fan, and element is never washable. Wipe only.

Wear parts and when to replace them

The crisper plate and basket coating are the consumable parts of an air fryer. Most major brands sell replacement baskets and plates for far less than a new unit, so a shedding plate is a part swap, not a replacement purchase. If coating flakes appear in food, stop using that part until it is replaced.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my air fryer smoke?

Almost always accumulated grease on the element or below the crisper plate, or a very fatty food dripping onto a hot surface. Clean the coil area and cavity, and add a tablespoon of water under the basket when cooking bacon or wings.

Can I put the basket in the dishwasher?

Many are rated for it, but hand washing preserves the nonstick coating far longer. If you do use the dishwasher, use the top rack and a gentle cycle.

The coating is flaking—is it dangerous?

Small flakes passing through are generally considered inert, but a shedding surface only gets worse. Replace the basket or plate; most brands sell them separately.

How do I get rid of the new-appliance smell?

Run the empty unit at around 400 degrees for ten minutes in a ventilated kitchen once or twice. The protective manufacturing oils burn off and the smell disappears.
